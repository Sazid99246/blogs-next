# Welcome to Bloglist

A place to discover, share, and keep track of interesting blogs.

## What is Bloglist?

Bloglist is a blogging application where users can share interesting articles and websites with others. You can explore blogs published by other users, interact with them, and maintain your own reading list.

## Features

- **Browse blogs:** Explore blogs shared by other users.
- **Create blogs:** Share interesting blogs with the community.
- **Like blogs:** Show appreciation for blogs you find interesting.
- **Reading list:** Save blogs you want to read later.
- **Track your reading:** Mark blogs as read when you finish reading them.
- **User profiles:** View users and their published blogs.

## Get started

Create an account or log in to start exploring Bloglist.
